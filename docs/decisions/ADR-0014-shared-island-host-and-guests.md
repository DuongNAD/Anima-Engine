## Context & Owner-Confirmed First-Milestone Decisions (`QD-SHAREDISLAND`, `2026-11-24`)

Prior multi-machine notes split into two axes: batch worker compute (`Contribute`) and per-device independent islands (`Inhabit`, [`ADR-0010`](ADR-0010-pinned-world-artifact.md), [`2026-08-25-feature-federated-compute.md`](../ai/design/2026-08-25-feature-federated-compute.md), [`DIRECTION_AND_COORDINATION_PLAN_2026-11-13.md`](../planning/DIRECTION_AND_COORDINATION_PLAN_2026-11-13.md) §5 Stage 7 & §6 `NM-4` / `QĐ-M7`). Cross-OS world-checksum measurements (`determinism_matrix.md`, [`ADR-0008`](ADR-0008-world-past-and-time.md)) showed that live Bevy worlds diverge across OSes around tick `180`, making multi-machine lockstep co-simulation unviable over residential networks and keeping lockstep **rejected**.

On `2026-11-24` (**`QD-SHAREDISLAND`**, confirmed by the owner), the project's primary product goal was set to **many machines inside one shared island**: one **host** runs the authoritative simulation and holds world truth, while **guests** join over the network to watch or inhabit a creature. Because only the host simulates, cross-OS floating-point differences between host and guest hardware cannot split simulation truth. **`QD-SHAREDISLAND` amends `QD-SCOPE`** ([`AGENT_WORKFLOW.md:314`](../how-to/AGENT_WORKFLOW.md#L314), un-pausing `Inhabit` federation) and is **distinct from `QD-ISLAND`** ([`AGENT_WORKFLOW.md:310`](../how-to/AGENT_WORKFLOW.md#L310), the `2026-11-19` island-rule science question, which remains unchanged).

**Decisions already confirmed by the owner for Milestone 1 (DECIDED, not open defaults):**

1. **Host-only authoritative simulation:** Exactly one host simulates the island; guests never simulate world state or step physics.
2. **LAN only in Milestone 1:** No public Internet exposure until authentication and TLS exist (`ISLAND-D7` stays an open owner D-slot for the later Internet milestone).
3. **Guest capabilities (`Spectate` and single-organism `Inhabit`):** Guests may watch (`Spectate`) or inhabit one **animal or individual plant** (tree or shrub, `ADR-0015`; grass cells remain non-inhabitable populations in Milestone 1 per `D-PLANT-1`). Every guest action reaches the host strictly as an intent message converted into a declared `CauseId` intervention ([`ADR-0004`](ADR-0004-observer-as-declared-intervention.md)) recorded in the host's World Record ([`ADR-0008`](ADR-0008-world-past-and-time.md)). Plants and grass cells can always be watched and followed in the chronicle (`§6`).
4. **Design first, other tracks continue:** `ADR-0014` and the ordered implementation card plan precede implementation; parallel project lanes continue unchanged (`Map v2` on macOS, `SEL2-P2` on Windows 1, with `S7` deferred rather than cancelled).
5. **`QD-GENESIS` three-phase organism genesis (`DECIDED` `2026-11-24`):** `G1` fixed founders for the Milestone 1 demo (`live.founder_genotype = 1.0` 2-segment animal + assigned `live.predator_fraction`, plus one fixed grass, shrub, and tree genotype once `ADR-0015` lands), `G2` screened founder pool upgrade (producers first, then herbivores, then predators + multi-pool robustness check in Card `I11`), and `G3` time-boxed emergent primordial research spike (opt-in world law, separate ADR, explicit non-chemistry boundary). Every run records its genesis phase in the genesis manifest, `world_identity`, and `ExperimentManifest::fingerprint()`, and experiments never mix phases.
6. **`QD-PLAYRULES` (`DECIDED` `2026-11-24`, resolved into ADR rather than left as open D-slots):**
   - **Time scale & speed limit:** Shared-island creature lifespan is `~1–2 hours` of real time via a declared `"real-time ecology profile"` in the world law (lifespan, maturation, gestation in ticks), pinned in the island manifest while science runs keep their own profiles; faster-than-real-time play is allowed **only when zero guests are connected** (prehistory, empty island). Day/night cycle (`D-TIME-1`) and season length (`D-TIME-2`) remain open D-slots constrained to fit this lifespan (lead suggestion: one day `~20 minutes`).
   - **Inhabited organism death (no respawn):** Body remains as a carcass and decomposes (`V4`, [`ADR-0013:40`](ADR-0013-live-creature-lifecycle.md#L40), [`agent_systems.rs:135,1447`](../../src-tauri/src/core/agent_systems.rs#L135-L1447)); guest reverts to `Spectate` at the death site and may inhabit one of its own living offspring, a nearby young member of the same lineage, or another host-allowed organism with zero carryover.
   - **Total extinction:** If all life goes extinct, the island does **not** auto-revive; the chronicle records extinction; the host explicitly opens a **new era** (`era_index`, [`world_record/types.rs:157`](../../src-tauri/src/core/world_record/types.rs#L157)) from genesis (`G1`/`G2`) or an earlier checkpoint.
   - **Guest power & host moderation:** A guest controls **one organism and IS strictly that organism** (identical body, senses, energy, and limits; zero extra speed, strength, sight, or immunity); environment interventions (rain, drought, etc.) are **host-only**; the host may remove (kick/disconnect) any guest.
   - **Scale foundations:** Few users now, build foundations for later — assign/persist stable `guest_id` and `island_id`, keep interest management (stream only nearby state), bound & compact records, and never assume one guest; implement for a small LAN group first.
   - **Reproduction & communication boundary (`ADR-0001` & `ADR-0003` unchanged):** Sexual reproduction with two sexes, signal/proximity mate finding, homologous recombination / compatibility drop (`EnergyPathwayGenotype::crossover` `None`, [`evolution_pathway.rs:41,160`](../../src-tauri/src/core/evolution_pathway.rs#L41-L160); `crossover_genotypes` [`crossover.rs:32`](../../src-tauri/src/evolution/crossover.rs#L32)) -> reproductive isolation -> biological species (`AE-S11`/`AE-S14`, enabling `ADR-0016` allopatric speciation) goes into the `ADR-0013` amendment (`D13`); plant hermaphroditism, outcrossing, selfing inbreeding cost, and clonal grass spread go into `ADR-0015` (`D14`); evolved multi-channel communication (`QD-SIGNALS`) goes into `ADR-0017` (`D18`).

Relationship to [`ADR-0010`](ADR-0010-pinned-world-artifact.md) and `QĐ-M7`: the host uses the pinned `WorldIdentity` world artifact as the shared terrain truth loaded identically by host and guests (`ADR-0010`), while redirecting the `Inhabit` product focus from disconnected per-device islands to a single host-authoritative shared island (leaving `Contribute` batch workers unaffected).

## Architecture Decisions & Open Owner D-Slots

### 1. Authority, Intent Seam, Guest Power Limits & Host Moderation

- **Strict server authority & host-only environment interventions:** The host executes the sole ECS world and physics/metabolism tick loop (`build_tick_schedule` at [`simulation_schedule.rs:121`](../../src-tauri/src/core/simulation_schedule.rs#L121)). Guests send typed **intent** messages (`GuestIntentMessage`: handshake/join with stable `guest_id` and `island_id` check, camera interest window update for `Spectate`, `ClaimOrganism` / `ReleaseOrganism`, animal `CreatureControl`, individual-plant `PlantControl` [root/canopy/defence/reproduction allocation, flower/seed timing, seed count vs. seed size within genotype limits, *to be wired alongside `ADR-0015`*] — `to be created by I1`/`I4`). Per `QD-PLAYRULES` Rule 5 (`DECIDED`), environment interventions (rain, drought, food additions, lighting changes) are strictly **host-only** (`GuestIntentMessage` omits environment/macro interventions completely). Guests can **never** send raw state packets (`SegmentState`, positions, energy, `SavedSimulationState`, or `AgentMigrationData`), overwrite genotypes, trigger instant growth, or move stationary plants; any unsolicited, unauthorized-intervention, or state-mutating payload is rejected at decode before touching simulation channels and counted in host diagnostics.
- **Exact organism parity — zero extra guest power (`DECIDED`, `QD-PLAYRULES` Rule 5):** A guest controls one organism and **is strictly that organism**: identical body morphology, sensors, metabolic costs, energy reserves, and physical limits as any autonomous creature or plant of the same lineage/genotype. The host grants zero extra speed, strength, sight range, energy, or immunity to inhabited entities.
- **Per-guest `CauseId` attribution & stable IDs (`to be created by I1`/`I2`):** A single `CAUSE_OBSERVER = u32::MAX` ([`causal.rs:30`](../../src-tauri/crates/anima-domain/src/causal.rs#L30)) cannot separate multiple guests. Following `QD-PLAYRULES` Rule 6 (scale foundations), every island assigns a stable `island_id` and every guest uses a stable `guest_id` mapped to a reserved contiguous `CauseId` band `ISLAND_GUEST_CAUSE_BASE..CAUSE_OBSERVER` (`to be created by I1`, rejected inside preregistered manifests via [`is_reserved_cause`](../../src-tauri/crates/anima-domain/src/causal.rs#L37)) and recorded in the island session directory (`(guest_id, cause_id, role, join_tick, leave_tick)` in `WorldRecordHeader`, `to be created by I2`).
- **Deterministic ingress & physical/EU grounding (`to be created by I1`/`I2`):** Async WebSocket tasks validate frame bounds and enqueue parsed intents into a bounded host ingress queue (`SharedGuestIntents`, `to be created by I1`). At a fixed pre-sensory slot beside [`drain_observer_actions_system`](../../src-tauri/src/core/observer.rs#L627) ([`simulation_schedule.rs:135`](../../src-tauri/src/core/simulation_schedule.rs#L135)), the host stamps each accepted intent with `(apply_tick, seq, cause_id)` and updates the target organism's `InhabitedControl` component (`to be created by I2`, zero heap allocations in the tick). At [`action_resolution_system`](../../src-tauri/src/core/agent_systems.rs#L1911) ([`simulation_schedule.rs:139`](../../src-tauri/src/core/simulation_schedule.rs#L139)), leased **animals** accept intent overrides into `cpg_parameters` and `ActionGates` (`pheromone_emit`, `attack_intent`, `feed_intent`, [`components.rs:501-532`](../../src-tauri/src/core/components.rs#L501-L532)) while holding `LastTransitionState` ([`agent_systems.rs:1962-1965`](../../src-tauri/src/core/agent_systems.rs#L1962-L1965)) paused; and at [`steering_drive_system`](../../src-tauri/src/core/locomotion.rs#L190) (`:318`, running 4 systems later at [`simulation_schedule.rs:143`](../../src-tauri/src/core/simulation_schedule.rs#L143)), `InhabitedControl` overrides/bypasses autonomous target selection into `InertiaComponent::target_velocity` ([`components.rs:464-465`](../../src-tauri/src/core/components.rs#L464-L465)) so steering never gets overwritten on the same tick, never bypasses `total_cost` metabolic energy expenditure ([`agent_systems.rs:1289-1293`](../../src-tauri/src/core/agent_systems.rs#L1289-L1293)), and never injects human policy into the neural learner. Once evolving plant organisms land (`ADR-0015`), leased **individual plants** route `PlantControl` intents cleanly through the closed-EU plant energy ledgers within genotype limits (no Lamarckian inheritance — guest allocation choices die with the plant; seeds inherit genotype only per [`ADR-0003`](ADR-0003-evolved-per-agent-brains.md)). Grass cells are populations, never individuals (`D-PLANT-1`), and remain non-inhabitable unless a future owner-approved patch mode lands.
- **Inhabited entity LOD exemption (`to be created by I2`):** Multi-guest cameras never write `SharedLodFocus` ([`simulation_lod.rs:90`](../../src-tauri/src/core/simulation_lod.rs#L90)); inhabited animals remain `Hot` solely via their explicit `InhabitedControl` lease while every other entity follows `Absent`/`Spectate` fairness.
- **Disconnect, host kick & inhabited-death behavior (`QD-PLAYRULES` Rules 3 & 5 DECIDED; `to be created by I2`/`I4`):**
  - *Disconnect or host moderation removal:* When a guest disconnects (clean close or missed keepalive timeout) or is explicitly **removed (kicked) by the host**, the host revokes the session token, closes its socket if still open, enqueues an authoritative `ReleaseOrganism { cause_id }` intent at the next tick boundary, returns the organism cleanly to autonomous AI/plant control with **zero EU created, destroyed, or shifted to detritus**, and records the release in the World Record.
  - *Inhabited organism death (`DECIDED`, `QD-PLAYRULES` Rule 3):* **No respawn.** The dead body stays in the world as a carcass and decomposes through [`ReclaimAndDespawnAgentCommand`](../../src-tauri/src/core/agent_systems.rs#L135) (`:1447`) / the `V4` carcass decomposer pipeline ([`ADR-0013:40`](ADR-0013-live-creature-lifecycle.md#L40)). The guest automatically reverts to `Spectate` at the death location, and may then submit a new `ClaimOrganism` for one of its own living offspring, a nearby young member of the same lineage, or another host-allowed organism; **zero state, stats, items, or learned weights carry over**.
  - **`ISLAND-D1` (Open Owner Decision — Disconnect Grace Period):** *(EMPTY — awaiting owner decision)* Should unexpected network disconnect release the organism **(a)** immediately after keepalive timeout (`Recommended`, prevents zombie stall while returning AI control cleanly) or **(b)** hold an idle lease for a reconnect grace window (`N` seconds, e.g., `10 s`) matching stable `guest_id` token before autonomous release? (Explicit host removal always releases immediately.)
  - **`ISLAND-D2` (Open Owner Decision — Possession Exclusivity & Host Claim Eligibility Policy):** *(EMPTY — awaiting owner decision; death -> no respawn/`V4` carcass/`Spectate` at death site is already DECIDED above)* **(a)** Strict 1-guest-per-organism first-claim lock until explicit release, kick, timeout, or death, allowing guests to re-inhabit any host-allowed organism (`Recommended`: own offspring / nearby young lineage member first, or any eligible organism), vs **(b)** host preempt override capable of reassigning a lease?

### 2. Streaming, Animation & Camera While Inhabiting

- **Off-tick host projection (`to be created by I3`):** The host never renders for guests. Terrain matches by pinned `WorldIdentity` ([`world_artifact.rs:122,136`](../../src-tauri/src/core/world_artifact.rs#L122-L136)) exchanged in the join handshake so guests load the static map artifact locally (or stream static terrain tiles once on join if using generated terrain). An off-tick projection thread (replacing full [`SavedSimulationState`](../../src-tauri/src/core/simulation_state.rs#L459), [`snapshot.rs:55-85`](../../src-tauri/src/core/snapshot.rs#L55-L85), which leaks `SimRng`/weights up to `MAX_SNAPSHOT_BYTES = 256 MiB`) sends:
  1. One stripped `IslandJoinSnapshot` (`island_id`, `tick`, `world_checksum`, `world_identity`, static `EnvironmentalState` trees/lakes, initial active creatures, lineage summary, island chronicle).
  2. Periodic `IslandSnapshot` keyframes / `IslandDelta` frames (`to be created by I3`) scoped by the guest's **interest area** (camera viewport or inhabited creature neighborhood), carrying `tick`, monotonic `seq`, delta-compressed creature segment poses, delta `EnvironmentalState` resource amounts, sparse `CombatEvent`/`WorldEvent` notifications, and `PheromoneGridState` quantized/throttled separately (per `C11` sizing, eliminating redundant `head_directions` and full `35 KB` static `environmental_state` JSON resends per frame).
  - **`ISLAND-D3` (Open Owner Decision — Stream Framing, Rate & LAN Guest Cap):** *(EMPTY — awaiting owner decision)* **(a)** `bincode` binary `IslandSnapshot`/`IslandDelta` frames (`quantized f16/i16 delta poses`, ~`20 Hz` default stream cadence, `Recommended` — `C11` computes ~`1.44–2.16 MB/s` vs `36–53 MB/s` for `1,000`-agent JSON at `30 Hz`), default LAN cap `16` concurrent guests (`MAX_MIGRATION_WEBSOCKET_CONNECTIONS = 64` ceiling, [`networking_systems.rs:27`](../../src-tauri/src/core/networking_systems.rs#L27)); vs **(b)** JSON frames (simpler debugging, lower agent/guest headroom); vs **(c)** `30 Hz` stream cadence (`localhost` / wired LAN).

- Shared-island sessions (`Spectate` or `Inhabit` with network listeners) run under declared observer policies ([`ObserverPolicy`](../../src-tauri/src/core/observer.rs#L49)); sessions with inhabited guests are **separate `Inhabit` treatments** ([`is_comparable_to_headless == false`](../../src-tauri/src/core/observer.rs#L88)) and may never be mixed into preregistered confirmation ensembles (`E3b`, `SEL2-P2`, `LAW1`).

- Host and guest UIs display a clear treatment banner whenever a run is a shared/inhabited island (`WorldRecordHeader` marks shared-island metadata explicitly) alongside the active genesis label (`"designed founders"` for `G1`).
