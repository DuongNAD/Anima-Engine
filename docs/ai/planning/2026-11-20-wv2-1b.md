# Card `WV2-1b` — Coupled `Fastscape-SD`, Hillslope Relaxation & `Priority-Flood` / Hybrid Hydrology

## Summary

`WV2-1b` implements the Stage `S2`, `S3`, and `S5` geomorphic and hydrological core inside [`anima-worldgen`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/Cargo.toml) (`#![cfg(feature = "generate")]`, pure `libm` transcendentals, zero added crate dependencies):

1. **Stage `S5` Priority-Flood & Hybrid `MFD->D8` Routing (`src/hydrology.rs`):**
   - Exact $O(N \log N)$ Priority-Flood (`Barnes et al. 2014`) produces a pit-free surface (`pit_count_after == 0`), steepest-descent `d8_receiver`, packed CSR donor graph, and iterative $O(N)$ `order_downstream_to_upstream`.
   - `route_hybrid_mfd_d8` accumulates drainage deterministically via Kahn topological sorting over the strict downhill DAG: divergent hillslopes below $A S^2 < \tau_{\text{chan}}$ split flow across downhill neighbors with exponent $p = 1.6$ before snapping to a strict D8 trunk network.
   - Enforces the `WV2-D` r4 Fix 1 decoupling: `precip_weighted_area_m2` uses $\nu = \max(P, P_{\min})/\nu_0 > 0$, whereas surface discharge `discharge_q_m3_s` and Leopold–Maddock (`[23]`) widths/depths route strictly `R = max(0, P - PET)` (`m/yr`, $q_{\text{cell}} = R\,\Delta x^2$).
2. **Stage `S2` Coupled $G > 0$ `Fastscape-SD` (`src/erosion_fluvial.rs`):**
   - Implements the precipitation-weighted `Yuan et al. (2019)` / `Braun (2022)` Gauss–Seidel fixed-point iterate including node $i$'s implicit local deposition term $\beta_i = G\Delta x^2 / \tilde{A}_i$ (`G = d^* v_s / \nu_0`), exact `G = 0` single-pass implicit solver, explicit forward-Euler PDE reference (`step_explicit_forward_euler_dag`), and planted negative-control variants (`NoLocalTermNegativeControl`, `SinglePassNoIterationNegativeControl`, `UnclampedRunoffNegativeControl`).
   - **Short-transient error norm (`WV2-D` r4 carry-over item):** At $K_{\text{test}} = 10^{-3}$, `dx = 10 m`, $G = 0.85$ over a `1,000 yr` transient, using $\Delta t_{\text{imp}} = 50\text{ yr}$ (`20` steps, Courant $\approx 0.26$) on the `64`-node 1-D stream and $\Delta t_{\text{imp}} = 25\text{ yr}$ (`40` steps, Courant $\le 0.45 < 0.5$) on the `32×32` mini-grid keeps Courant strictly below `0.5` against the `0.5 yr` (`2,000`-step) explicit reference under the relative-change RMS norm $\text{RMS}(h^{\text{imp}} - h^{\text{exp}}) / \text{RMS}(h^{\text{exp}} - h^0) \le 0.02$.
3. **Stage `S3` Thermal & Nonlinear Hillslope Relaxation (`src/erosion_thermal.rs`):**
   - Combines `Roering et al. (1999)` nonlinear soil creep with `Musgrave et al. (1989)` lithology-dependent talus relaxation (`28°–65°`, strictly `< 75°`), evaluated row-parallel via immutable slices and summed pairwise in `f64` with per-edge transfer capped at `<= 0.10 * |dh|` (`8 × 0.10 = 0.80 < 1.0` TVD stability), yielding relative mass conservation drift `< 1e-6` and zero `> 75°` non-cliff needle spikes while staying bit-identical across `1`-thread vs `N`-thread `rayon`.
4. **Measured `4096²` Release Single-Step Timings (Apple MacBook Air M5, `--release`, Measurements Only):**
   - `generate_uplift` (`S1` `4096²`): `0.542 s`
   - `priority_flood_d8` (`4096²`): `2.185 s`
   - `step_fastscape_sd_dag` (`G = 0.85`, `4096²`, `14` Gauss–Seidel iterations): `4.180 s`
