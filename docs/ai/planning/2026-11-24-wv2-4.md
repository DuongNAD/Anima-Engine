### 2.2 Deterministic 5-Level Selection Rule

1. **Level 1 (`overall_pass`):** Prefer candidates with `overall_pass == true` (all 15 gates pass).
2. **Level 2 (`passed_gate_count`):** Higher count of passing gates out of `15` (`8` `G-SHAPE` + `5` `G-COMP v2` + `2` `G-SIM` raw checks).
3. **Level 3 (Hard Invariants):** Prefer `G-SIM` passing AND zero unmodeled micro-pits, zero severed humid spillways, zero non-cliff needle spikes, and `0` cells in `Chaparral`/`Steppe`.
4. **Level 4 (Coverage & Shortfall Tie-Break):** Higher `zoned_16_biomes_passing` count (`0..=16`), then lower sum of relative shortfalls on failing numeric gate bounds.
5. **Level 5 (Deterministic Tie-Break):** Lowest pre-registered candidate ID (`C01 < C02 < ... < C08`).

**Stage-2 Promotion:** Promote the top `2` ranked Stage-1 (`1024²`) candidates verbatim (preserving seed and preset parameters, setting `grid_size = 4096`) to full `4096²` generation + verification, recording wall time and peak RSS. Official `artifacts/world-v2/{preset_v2.toml,sim_256.anmw3,index.json,gates.json}` is written only if the top Stage-2 (`4096²`) candidate passes `15/15` gates (`overall_pass == true`).

## 4. Root-Cause Diagnosis & Owner Decision Options (`GATE, BLOCKED`)

Per `§2.2` and `MAP-D3`, official `artifacts/world-v2/` is held **uncommitted** (zero verifier thresholds were altered) and the card enters **`GATE, BLOCKED`**. Archived machine-readable Stage-1/Stage-2 JSON/TOML outputs live in hub folder `test_runs/anima-mac_WV2-4_artifacts/`.

1. **Channel network truncation (`omega_max = 3`) cascade:** Active channels with `Q >= 0.027_778 m³/s` (`w >= 0.5 m`) cover only `~0.024%–0.025%` of map area (`0.56–0.57 km/km²`, `41,682–43,487` cells), capping Strahler order at `3` and longest mainstem at `7.96–9.89 km`. That single upstream hydrological shortfall drives failures across `G-SHAPE.1`, `G-SHAPE.2` (few rain-shadow junctions), `G-SHAPE.3` (`D_d`), `G-SHAPE.4` (Hack `h`, `R²`), `G-SHAPE.5` (`downstream_samples = 0`), `C4` (`river_frac`, `w2 < w3 < w4`), and `GS-1` (`strahler_ge4_blocks = 0`).
2. **Zero `4096²` waterfalls (`wf = 0`):** Steep caprock drops formed cleanly in Stage 1 (`wf = 5` on `C01`) either erode below the `12 m` drop / `< 0.08` upstream-slope threshold during `4096²` fine steps or fail knickpoint detection at `10 m` spacing (`MAP-D3`).
3. **Under-represented lakes (`lake_frac = 0.000256..0.000716` vs `[0.008, 0.045]`):** Even though `C08` reaches `large_lakes = 5`, total lake area is `~11×–31×` below `C3`'s floor (`MAP-D3`).
4. **Over-smooth coastline & zero coastal cliffs (`cliff = 0.0`, `D_shore ~ 0.99`, `shoreline_cells = 275..332`):** The Lost Coast escarpment fails to produce `>= 10%` steep (`>= 0.35` slope) coastline at `10 m` resolution, and smooth coastal geometry keeps `256²` walkable shoreline count below `500` (`GS-2`).
5. **Fine-scale `4096²` arête spikes (`non_cliff_needle_spikes = 4,642..5,076`):** Thermal/hillslope diffusion keeps `1024²` spike-free (`0` on `C01..C04, C06..C08`), yet leaves `~4.6k–5.1k` isolated `> 75°` Laplacian-reversal spikes on non-cliff slopes at `dx = 10 m` (`G-SHAPE.7`).

### Owner Decision Options
1. **Option 1 — Rework `WV2-2` generator physics first:** Leave all `G-SHAPE` / `G-COMP v2` / `G-SIM` gates untouched; schedule a follow-up `WV2-2` physics card addressing channel runoff/initiation (`omega_max >= 4`, `D_d`, `Hack`), knickpoint waterfalls at `4096²`, basin lake area, coastal cliff roughness, and `10 m` arête smoothing before generating and committing `artifacts/world-v2/`.
2. **Option 2 — Formal design amendment:** Amend the failing `§4.1`, `§4.2`, `§5.1`, and `§5.2` target bounds in [the World v2 design](../design/2026-11-20-world-v2-design.md) via owner-approved design delta, then re-verify the pre-registered `C01`/`C08` outputs against the amended thresholds.
3. **Option 3 — Accept `MAP-D3` shortfalls & commit a provisional `artifacts/world-v2/`:** Explicitly authorize committing either **`C01`** (`2/15` pass: `G-SHAPE.6` `0.0` endorheic + `C2`; `15/16` zoned biomes [`Taiga` short at `131` conn], `2` large lakes) or **`C08`** (`1/15` pass: `C2`; `16/16` zoned biomes [`Taiga` `4,128` conn], `5` large lakes, `0.000183` humid endorheic fraction) into `artifacts/world-v2/{preset_v2.toml,sim_256.anmw3,index.json,gates.json}` and proceeding with Windows `anima-case` `sim_256.anmw3` byte-identity verification.
