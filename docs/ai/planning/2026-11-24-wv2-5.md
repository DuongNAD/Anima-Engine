# Card `WV2-5` — Offline Bake Engine, Grid Scripts & `QD-WV25` Gate Closure

## 1. Implementation Scope

Implements the offline bake codebase for `WV2-5` (Card 6 of [the World v2 design](../design/2026-11-20-world-v2-design.md)) and closes owner decision `QD-WV25` inside [`anima-worldgen`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/Cargo.toml) (`#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, `#![deny(clippy::disallowed_methods)]` enforcing `libm`-only transcendentals):

- `src/bake/mod.rs`: shared `256 × 256` active + `2`-texel bilinear overlap gutter (`260 × 260`, `65 × 65` `4 × 4` blocks) pyramid geometry, `L0..L4` mip sizing, canonical macro-albedo derivation shared by CPU/GPU lighting and `macro_albedo`, strip/range CLI parsers, and border-overlap checks.
- `src/bake/fields.rs` (`JOB-W3`): central-difference `normal_xy`, exact **Meijster (2000)** 2-pass $O(N)$ squared Euclidean Distance Transform producing clamped `[-500, +500] m` `shore_sdf` + coastal wave exposure, 2-cell waterfall-halo `hydro_flow`, and `--tile-strip S/K` sharding (`merge_field_strips`) byte-identical to global execution.
- `src/bake/flora_place.rs`: deterministic per-`256²` sector decorative vegetation placement (`ANMF` v1 binary, packed `8`-byte records with mandatory `FLORA_FLAG_DECORATIVE`, `ADR-0010` §4).
- `src/bake/lighting_cpu.rs` (`JOB-W4 --backend cpu`): exact bounded-radius (`256` cells) 8-azimuth Stewart (1998) blocked upper-hull horizon sweep with 16-cell max pruning, producing `(ao, svf, bounce_luma)` (`terrain_lighting`), 4-azimuth directional horizon elevation angles `(theta_E, theta_W, theta_N, theta_S)` (`terrain_horizon`), `512²` sky/bounce probe, and bit-identical `--tile-range A..B` per-tile sharding.
- `src/bake/lighting_gpu.rs` (`#[cfg(feature = "gpu-bake")]`, `JOB-W0` + `JOB-W4 --backend wgpu`): `wgpu 30.0.2` adapter discovery (`probe-gpu`) and WGSL compute horizon shader parity within Card 6 tolerances (`max_abs_delta <= 2`, `rmse <= 0.4`, `ssim >= 0.999`).
- `src/bake/pack_ktx2.rs` (`JOB-W5`): pure-Rust BC4/BC5 encoder, KTX2 v2.0 container writer & parser (`ZLIB` supercompression, Khronos Basic DFD, KVD including `animaBorderF32` exact seam heights, internal mip chains for `512²` probe/impostors), `Block PSNR` & Wang (2004) `SSIM` quality evaluators, deterministic USTAR `.tar.gz` packager enforcing **zero `index.json` or `sim_256.anmw3` inside the archive (`LD-2`)**, and strict archive SHA-256 / KTX2 / `ANMC` / `ANMF` verifier.
- `src/bake/assets.rs` (`JOB-W6`): `QD-ASSETS` / `QD-WV25 (a)` `SOURCES.json` policy validator (`ambientCG` / `Poly Haven` only, `CC0-1.0`, `https://` `licence_url`, `<= 60 MB` download / `<= 20 MB` committed, pinned SHA-256), pure-Rust Wavefront `.obj` and binary glTF 2.0 (`.glb`, node hierarchy + TRS transforms) mesh parser, deterministic vertex-grid LOD simplifier (`ANMM` v1 `lod0..lod3`), and software octahedral hemisphere (`8 × 8` views) `RGBA8`+`BC5` impostor KTX2 baker.
- `src/bin/anima-bake.rs`: CLI exposing `probe-gpu`, `fields`, `lighting`, `pack`, `verify`, and `assets`.
- [`scripts/grid_bake_world_v2.sh`](../../../scripts/grid_bake_world_v2.sh): OxideSwarm grid runner enforcing `--worker desktop-3epv830`, `WORK_PLAN.md` `GRID: PAUSED` check, worker readiness & `anima-case` non-overlap check, `JOB-W0` GPU pre-probe, and `GATE (a)` asset guard.
- [`tests/wv2_5_bake_tests.rs`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/tests/wv2_5_bake_tests.rs), [`tests/wv2_5_gpu_bake_tests.rs`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/tests/wv2_5_gpu_bake_tests.rs), and [`scripts/test_target_policy.mjs`](../../../scripts/test_target_policy.mjs): feature-gated targets for `bake` and `gpu-bake`.
