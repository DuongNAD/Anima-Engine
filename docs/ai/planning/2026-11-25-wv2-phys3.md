## 2. Mechanism Hypotheses (One per Target Gate)

1. **`G-SHAPE.3` (`caprock_tile_cv = 0.6953 -> <= 0.45`):**
   - *Diagnosis:* [`eval_metric_3_drainage_density`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/verify/g_shape.rs#L882-L936) measures `D_d` CV across `2 km × 2 km` tiles with `>= 80%` dry `SandstoneCaprock`. In [`tectonic.rs`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/tectonic.rs#L425-L512), `SandstoneCaprock` spans both gently sloping interior plateau tiles (`S ~ 0.01–0.03`) and steeper flank/bench tiles (`S ~ 0.08–0.18`). Under `A * S² >= channel_init`, `S²` varies by `~20–100×` between interior and flank caprock tiles, starving interior tiles of initiated channels.
   - *Hypothesis:* Lowering `channel_init` (e.g. `100 -> 45..70`) together with a steeper, more uniform east-tilted caprock tableland gradient (`220 -> 300..360 m` across `plateau_u - u` in `eval_cell_raw`) and reduced flanking stratigraphic `SandstoneCaprock` slope contrast initiates channels earlier and more uniformly across all `>= 80%` caprock tiles, reducing `caprock_tile_cv` below `0.45` while keeping `global_dd in [0.8, 3.5]` and `dissection_ratio >= 1.5`.

2. **`G-SHAPE.4` & `C4` (`Hack R² = 0.6136 -> >= 0.85`):**
   - *Diagnosis:* [`eval_metric_4_hack_law`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/verify/g_shape.rs#L953-L1070) fits `ln(L_km)` vs `ln(A_km2)` across up to `2,000` `Strahler >= 2` channel cells (`0.02 < A <= 300 km²`, `L > 0.05 km`). On steep mountain chutes (`S ~ 0.4`), `A * S² >= channel_init` triggers at tiny drainage area (`A_init ~ 400 m²`), producing long `L` at small `A`, whereas on gentle piedmont/plateau slopes (`S ~ 0.02`), `is_channel` starts mid-basin (`L = dx` at `A_init ~ 0.1–0.3 km²`), creating two offset `(ln A, ln L)` branches and depressing `R²` to `0.614`.
   - *Hypothesis:* Reducing `channel_init` (`40..65`) and sharpening `mfd_p` (`1.30 -> 1.12..1.20`) shrinks the slope-dependent `A_init` lag on low-gradient reaches and focuses tributary convergence, collapsing `(ln A, ln L)` dispersion so `R² >= 0.85` while keeping `h in [0.52, 0.62]`, `C_km in [1.0, 2.0]`, and `sinuosity >= 1.05` (which also closes `C4`).

3. **`G-SHAPE.5` (`b_down = 1.0734 -> [1.60, 3.50]`, `vf_down = 0.9872 -> >= 1.50`, `vf_ratio = 2.0622 -> >= 2.5`):**
   - *Diagnosis:* [`sample_valley_transect`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/verify/g_shape.rs#L1092-L1184) marches perpendicularly out to `1,200 m` from `Strahler >= 4` trunk cells, computing `vf = 2 * v_fw / (h_l + h_r)` (where floor width `v_fw` stops at `dz <= max(1.0, 0.08 * mean_wall_h)`) and OLS `b_down` over all positive `dz > 0.25 m`. In [`widen_trunk_alluvial_floodplains`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/pipeline.rs#L1170-L1305), the target `z_i + 0.36 * (perp / 65)^2.15` rises quickly past `floor_thresh` (`v_fw` stays narrow, `vf_down ~ 0.99`), only runs on `z < 800 m`, `slope < 0.14`, and stops carving when `diff >= 75 m` or beyond `w_fp <= 560 m`, letting outer linear hillsides pull `b_down` to `1.07`.
   - *Hypothesis:* Extending `widen_trunk_alluvial_floodplains` to a broader flat-bottomed alluvial trough (`r_ref ~ 110–130 m`, flatter inner floor exponent `2.45–2.75` with smaller coefficient `0.10–0.16`, wider `w_fp ~ 650–950 m`, raised `diff` fade cap `140 m`, and higher `slope < 0.20`, `z < 1,050 m` gating while preserving `strahler >= 1` channel cells) widens the flat valley floor (`vf_down >= 1.8`, `vf_ratio >= 2.8`) and brings the outer wall transect within `1,200 m` into the `b_down in [1.7, 2.6]` concave U-valley regime without perturbing `flint_concavity_theta` (`0.361`) or `b_up`/`vf_up`.

4. **`G-SHAPE.4_2` (`D_shore = 1.1349 -> [1.15, 1.45]`):**
   - *Diagnosis:* `S2_2` (`coast_frac_amp = 0.075`) reached `1.1493` (`0.0007` below `1.15`). [`inject_fine_littoral_ria`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/pipeline.rs#L1050-L1163) currently uses `amp_m = 14.5 * (coast_frac_amp / 0.055).min(1.5)` and `amp *= 0.74`.
   - *Hypothesis:* Slightly boosting fine littoral ria amplitude and high-octave persistence (`amp_m` base `17.5 m`, `amp *= 0.77`, `h_fade` width `13 -> 16 m`) with `coast_frac_amp in [0.068, 0.078]` raises box-counting perimeter scaling across `[2..64]` cleanly into `[1.17, 1.28]` while preserving `GraniteCrystalline` cliffs and `>= 0.5 km²` river mouths (`C5` intact).

5. **`G-COMP-v2.C3.Lakes` (`lake_surface_fraction = 0.0047 -> [0.008, 0.045]`, `endo_humid == 0.0`):**
   - *Diagnosis:* [`apply_late_quaternary_piedmont_subsidence`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/pipeline.rs#L843-L1045) forms `<= 6` basins of half-axes `920 × 1,650 m * sqrt(basin_amp)`, yielding `0.0047` of total map area (`4096²`).
   - *Hypothesis:* Enlarging piedmont basin half-axes (`rx_m = 1,350 * b_scale`, `ry_m = 2,400 * b_scale`) and adding 2 intermediate piedmont/foot-slope candidate sites (`8` slots with `min_dist >= 3.5 km`) within the same `h in [10, 220] m`, `precip >= 1.45`, `drop_m <= 38 m`, `acc_cells < trunk_cells` safety bounds raises `lake_surface_fraction` to `~0.011–0.020` of total map area while retaining `Fill-Spill-Merge` outlets (`endo_humid == 0.0`).

### Round-1 X-Ray Diagnoses Motivating Round-2 (`Q07..Q12`)

1. **`mfd_p` dispersion inversion (`G-SHAPE.1 R_l`, `G-SHAPE.4 h` & `Hack R²`):** In `hydrology.rs:723`, MFD weights are `s^p`. Lowering `mfd_p` from `1.90` to `1.12–1.22` in Round-1 *dispersed* overland drainage instead of concentrating it, lowering `R_l` (`1.54 -> 1.30–1.38`), `h` (`0.478 -> 0.418–0.473`), and creating `176/477` sheet-flow cells in flat caprock tiles. **Fix:** restore `mfd_p in [1.95, 2.40]`.
2. **Stage-S4 `strahler` mismatch (`G-SHAPE.5 b_down`):** In [`pipeline.rs#L1954-L1960`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/pipeline.rs#L1954-L1960), `widen_trunk_alluvial_floodplains` built `q_channel` solely from `q >= 0.05` whereas [`g_shape.rs#L356-L392`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/verify/g_shape.rs#L356-L392) uses `(hydro.is_channel && q >= 0.05) || river_w_m >= 0.5` plus D8 bridging. Unskipped `Strahler >= 4` trunks hit `b_down = 1.51..2.39` and `vf = 3.49..23.79`, while skipped trunks pulled the median down to `0.74–1.15`. **Fix:** align `pipeline.rs` `q_channel` + D8 bridging identically to `g_shape.rs` and widen trunk gating to `z < 1,300 m, slope < 0.25`.
3. **Plateau `2.9 km` domes & `endo_humid` pit (`G-SHAPE.3 caprock_tile_cv`, `G-SHAPE.6`):** `58.0 * fbm_detail` (`14.0` frequency ~ `2.9 km`) in `tectonic.rs:425` created `2 km` tile domes (`D_d = 0.24`) and closed `967–981 m` plateau bowls (`endo_humid = 0.00017..0.00053` in `Q04..Q06`), while `core_gate` (`massif_h - 2,400`) let `SandstoneCaprock` climb steep arid flanks (`slope = 0.59, P = 0.20`). **Fix:** replace `58 * fbm_detail` with `12 * fbm_detail + 14 * plateau_subtile (freq=48) + 280 * (plateau_u - u)` and tighten `core_gate` to `(massif_h - 1,750) / 220` (preserving `A1` `[0.09, 0.21]` caprock fraction).
4. **Arid-east `river_w_m` initiation lag & short `512²` evolution (`G-SHAPE.4 Hack R²`, `C4`):** Arid eastern channels lagged in crossing `river_w_m >= 0.5` (`A_init ~ 0.5–2.8 km²` vs `~0.09 km²` west), depressing `Hack R²` by `~0.14`, while weak transverse divides produced unbranched parallel chutes. **Fix:** raise `worldgen_hydro_config` `bankfull_q_mult` (`5.5 -> 7.5`), strengthen transverse massif spurs in `tectonic.rs`, and increase coarse `512²` fluvial steps (`65 -> 115`).
5. **Summit-clip audit (`MSG_20261125_020000`):** Round-1 `Q01..Q06` all hit `max(total_h_m) = 5,760.00–5,760.05 m` (`5,130–5,183` cells `> 5,700 m` at `1024²`) because S2 uplift accumulated above S1's `soft_bound_elevation`. **Fix:** adjust S1 `soft_bound_elevation` upper span `0.995 -> 0.935` (`~5,596 m` asymptote) and apply an isostatic uplift taper above `5,170 m` approaching `5,620 m` in `pipeline.rs` so `max(total_h_m) < 5,700.0 m` (`0` clipped cells).
