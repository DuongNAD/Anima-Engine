# E3b Experiment Runner (`C1. E3B-RUNNER`)

## 1. Why `run_e3_experiment.rs` Cannot Run E3b (Read-Only Finding)

Inspection of [run_e3_experiment.rs](../../../src-tauri/examples/run_e3_experiment.rs) and [e3_smoke.rs](../../../src-tauri/examples/e3_smoke.rs) against the frozen E3b fixtures (`src-tauri/tests/fixtures/experiments_e3b/`) and [verify_e3b_artifacts.mjs](../../../scripts/verify_e3b_artifacts.mjs) established four blocking incompatibilities:

1. **Preregistration filename hardcoded:** `run_e3_experiment.rs:47` pins `PREREG_FILE = "e3-preregistration.json"` (`e3_smoke.rs:54` also hardcodes `"e3-preregistration.json"`), rather than `"e3b-preregistration.json"`.
2. **Missing `live.mean_lifespan` column (`F-053`):** `run_e3_experiment.rs:58-67` omits `live.mean_lifespan` from `SERIES_OBSERVABLES`, whereas `verify_e3b_artifacts.mjs:863-869` and `1379-1382` require `live.mean_lifespan` to be read exclusively from `series.csv` (never from `final-observables.json`).
3. **Incompatible threshold and statistical schemas:** `run_e3_experiment.rs:503-595` parses E3 thresholds (`dz_min`, `relative_delta_min`) instead of E3b's `eps_primary`, `eps_sensitivity`, `sesoi_log_var_ratio`, `fwer_alpha`, `min_pi_ll`, `min_non_zero_pairs_r7`, log variance ratio $L_i$, Holm $m=3$, 90% TOST CI, Lee (2009) trimming bounds, `RMST`, `ln(births_B/births_A)` mediator regression, and the 4-label `R10` decision partition checked in `verify_e3b_artifacts.mjs:1384-1421`.
4. **Incompatible artifact schemas and missing blind smoke runner:** `run_e3_experiment.rs:2060` writes lowercase `"completed"` (verifier checks `"Completed"` at `884, 941`), writes `"final_observables"` at line `2142` (verifier reads `.observables` at `843, 1375`), keys contrasts by full names instead of `"H1" | "H2" | "H3"`, and provides no blind E3b `--smoke` mode.

Therefore Card **C1 (`E3B-RUNNER`)** is required. Dry `--help` check verbatim on `run_e3_experiment.exe`:

```text
$ run_e3_experiment.exe --help   (EXIT=1)
E3-X runner error: unknown flag '--help'
```

And on the new `run_e3b_experiment.exe`:

```text
$ run_e3b_experiment.exe --help  (EXIT=0)
usage: run_e3b_experiment (--smoke | --ensemble | --replay --arm <arm> --seed <seed> | --self-test) [--manifest-dir <dir>] [--smoke-dir <dir>] [--out <dir>] [--checkpoint-dir <dir>] [--jobs <n>]

$ run_e3b_experiment.exe --self-test  (EXIT=0)
run_e3b_experiment --self-test PASSED
```

Zero edits were made under `src-tauri/src/**`, `scripts/verify_e3b_artifacts.mjs`, `package.json`, or the frozen fixtures.

## 2. Runner Architecture ([run_e3b_experiment.rs](../../../src-tauri/examples/run_e3b_experiment.rs))

1. **Pre-run safety gates (`--smoke`, `--ensemble`, `--replay`):**
   - Refuses debug builds (`cfg!(debug_assertions)` / `RuntimeEnv::is_debug_build`).
   - Refuses if `ANIMA_WORLD_ARTIFACT` is set or `<temp_dir>/anima_world_artifact.anmw` exists (without deleting it).
   - Validates `e3b-preregistration.json` (`status == "FROZEN"`), confirmation `FactorDiff` paths (`U vs T`: `["interventions"]`; `U vs S` and `S vs T`: `["initial_conditions", "interventions"]`), and strict seed disjointness between `execution_order` and `smoke_seed`.
2. **Blind `--smoke` mode:**
   - Runs ONLY the three blind smoke manifests (`smoke_seed = 999953`) after rejecting any `"trait_mass"` observable in their manifests.
   - Evaluates `WP-2(a)` (`256x256` `ResourceField`, identical closed EU and `SimRng` streams at genesis), `WP-2(b1)` (gradient standing-crop shift `>= 10%`), `WP-2(b2)` (scarcity standing-crop match within `+/- 10%` with zero `Deforest` ledger events), and `WP-2(c)` (`detritus > 0` before and at `warmup_ticks`).
   - Writes `manifests/` (only the three blind smoke manifests), `smoke-series.csv`, `smoke-report.json` (`body_mass_consulted: false`), `provenance.json`, and `checksums.sha256`, followed by a recursive scan asserting zero `"trait_mass"` substrings anywhere inside the output directory.
3. **Resumable `--ensemble` mode:**
   - Requires a validated blind smoke record (`wp2_passed: true`, zero `"trait_mass"` substrings, intact `checksums.sha256`).
   - Executes `(seed, arm)` tasks via a scoped `--jobs <N>` worker pool assembled strictly in deterministic `(execution_order, ARM_ORDER)` sequence, with optional atomic `--checkpoint-dir` persistence outside `--out`.
   - Computes exact E3b summary statistics (`L_i`, Holm $m=3$, 90% TOST, Lee bounds, `RMST`, `ln(births_B/births_A)` OLS mediator, `R10`) and emits `provenance.json`, `runs.csv`, `series.csv`, `final-observables.json`, `effects.json`, `manifests/`, and `checksums.sha256`.
