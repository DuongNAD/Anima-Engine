## Fastscape-SD Solver Contract (`WV2-1c` Carry-Over Notes `N1` & `N2`)

Carried forward from [the `WV2-1c` card note](./2026-11-20-wv2-1c.md) for [`erosion_fluvial.rs`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/erosion_fluvial.rs):

- **Sweep-budget wall-time scaling (`N2`):** `FluvialParams::max_total_sweeps` (default `DEFAULT_MAX_TOTAL_SWEEPS = 4_096`) bounds the **count of Gauss-Seidel sweeps** spent across Phase A (receiver-first full-`dt` fast path) and Phase B (in-place upstream-to-downstream fallback with adaptive `dt` halving), not wall-clock duration. Because each sweep traverses all `N` cells of the D8 DAG, the elapsed time before `FluvialError::NonConvergence { budget_exhausted: true, .. }` is returned grows proportionally with grid size `N` (`4096²` performs `64x` the per-sweep work of `512²`). Callers running large grids must size `max_total_sweeps` and `dt_yr` knowing that a fixed sweep cap bounds iteration count, not constant wall-clock time.
- **Dry-zone timestep sizing (`N2`):** Dry and arid rain-shadow zones (precipitation near `min_precip_m_yr`, producing large local deposition coupling `beta_i = G * dx^2 / A_tilde_i`) typically fail Phase A convergence and fall back to Phase B adaptive `dt_yr` halving (up to `MAX_ALLOWED_DT_HALVINGS = 12`, `4,096` substeps). Excessively large `dt_yr` triggers deep halving cascades that exhaust `max_total_sweeps` quickly, so dry zones require a **moderate `dt_yr`** to converge cleanly well within the sweep budget.
- **Phase A budget enforcement & non-mutation (`N1`):** Both Phase A and Phase B check `sweeps_spent >= params.max_total_sweeps` before executing each sweep; exhausting the budget in either phase returns `Err(FluvialError::NonConvergence { budget_exhausted: true, total_sweeps, .. })` with `total_sweeps <= max_total_sweeps` and leaves the input elevation slice `h` unmodified. Phase A's share of the budget is pinned by `phase_a_sweep_budget_halts_loudly_without_mutating_h` in [`wv2_1b_solver_hydro_tests.rs`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/tests/wv2_1b_solver_hydro_tests.rs) (humid 64-node chain at `nu = 1.0`, `dt_yr = 10_000.0`, `max_total_sweeps = 5`).

#### Root Cause 1 — Exact Double-Evaporation Bug in [`lakes_falls.rs`](../../../src-tauri/crates/anima-worldgen/src/lakes_falls.rs)

Inspecting `lakes_falls.rs:698, 724-729, 782-793, 816-820` exposes a **mass-balance bug** on every closed basin whose catchment is small or mostly lake surface:
- Line `:698` sets local inflow for every cell (including submerged lake cells inside `cell_leaf != NO_RECEIVER`) to **net runoff** `q_loc = (P - PET).max(0.0) * dx^2` — i.e., **`PET` has already been subtracted once** from precipitation falling on the lake surface.
- Line `:789` then computes `evap_full = sum_{c <= spill} PET[c] * dx^2`, and `:819` requires `q_pos > evap_full` — **subtracting `PET` over the lake surface a SECOND time**!
- Algebraically, for a depression of area `A_lake` and dry catchment area `A_land = 0`, `q_pos > evap_full` reduces to `(P - PET) * A_lake > PET * A_lake`, which requires **`P > 2 * PET`** just to fill a shallow local bowl! Even at `P = 2.05 m/yr` against `PET = 1.35 m/yr` (`P / PET = 1.52`, strongly humid!), any depression without a large upstream land catchment yields `Q_in = 0.70 * A < E_full = 1.35 * A` and becomes an unphysical `EndorheicLake`!

**Mass-Conserving Fix (`§2.2.6` exact balance):** Route catchment runoff `R = max(0, P - PET)` from upland non-submerged cells into the lake while crediting direct lake-surface cells with **gross precipitation** `P * dx^2` against **single-counted lake evaporation** `PET * dx^2` (or equivalently subtract net `PET - P` once over water surface vs `max(0, P - PET)` over land). Then:
- Any depression whose mean `(P, PET)` has `P > PET` (`P/PET > 1.0`, plus any depression with humid inflow exceeding net lake evaporation `Q_land + A_lake * P > A_lake * PET`) strictly satisfies `Q_in > E_full` and spills continuously (`0` endorheic basins where `P > PET`).
- Existing arid test invariants in `wv2_2_climate_biome_cave_tests.rs:430-436` (`|A(h_eq) * PET - Q_in| / Q_in < 0.10`) remain 100% consistent (for dry `A_eq` cells `PET` vs gross `Q_in`, updating the arid equilibrium equation to `A(h_eq) * (PET - P_lake) == Q_land_in` or keeping gross `P_in` inside `Q_in`).

#### Root Cause 2 & Ambiguity in the `0.5 <= P/PET < 1.0` Dry Sub-Humid Zone (`Questions for Lead`)

Even after fixing double-`PET` subtraction, notice a structural mismatch between `§2.2.5`, `§2.2.6`, and `§4.1`:
- Currently `lakes_falls.rs:1136` counts a cell in `endorheic_humid_fraction` whenever `(precip / pet) >= 0.5`, matching the `"($P/\text{PET} \ge 0.5$)"` label next to `severed humid spillways` in `§4.1` line 350.
- However, UNEP / Middleton & Thomas (1992) defines `0.50 <= P/PET < 0.65` as **Dry Sub-Humid** (net annual water deficit `P < PET`), and in **`§2.2.5`** (line 192) & **`§2.2.6`** (line 203) the design sets runoff strictly to `R = max(0, P - PET)` — which is **identically `0.0 m/yr`** throughout **`0.5 <= P/PET < 1.0`**!
- Under `R = max(0, P - PET)` and `A(h_eq) * PET = Q_in`, any closed depression in a dry sub-humid rain-shadow transition belt (`0.5 <= P/PET < 1.0`) whose rim does not capture external `P > PET` runoff **physically equilibrates below sill as an `EndorheicLake`** because water-balance inflow is less than evaporation (`P < PET`, `Q_in < E_lake`). This is exactly why `biomes.rs` (`512²`) measured `endorheic_humid_fraction = 0.000749` and `wv2_2` (`256²`) measured `0.001340` after forced fill was removed (`18_MSG`).

**Two Specific Questions for the Lead (Before Step B Code):**

- **Q1 (Definition of "humid zone" for `endorheic_humid_fraction` vs `severed_humid_spillways`):**
  Does `endorheic_humid_fraction == 0.0` (`§4.1` "`0%` in humid zones") intend:
  - **(a) Keep threshold at `P/PET >= 0.5`** (`lakes_falls.rs:1136`), meaning `S1–S5` terrain/stratigraphy must keep the leeward `0.5 <= P/PET < 1.0` foothills **exorheically dissected** (or apply Q2's sub-annual runoff model) so every depression in `P/PET >= 0.5` drains or receives enough `P > PET` mountain inflow to spill; **OR**
  - **(b) Align `endorheic_humid_fraction` with water-balance humidity (`P > PET`, i.e., `P/PET >= 1.0`)**, matching `§2.2.5` (`R = max(0, P - PET)`) and `§2.2.6` (`Q_in > E_lake`), while keeping `P/PET >= 0.5` solely for `severed_humid_spillways`? *(No gate will be altered in code unless the lead explicitly authorizes `(b)`.)*
