## 3. Proposed Five Physics Rework Mechanisms

Every mechanism keeps `src/verify/**` unchanged, keeps `libm::*` determinism (`1`-thread == `N`-thread), and preserves all existing unit-test invariants (notably `wv2_1a_tectonic_tests`, `ocean_fraction in [0.40, 0.50]`, `erodibility contrast <= 8.0`, and `a1_caprock_fraction in [0.09, 0.21]`):

1. **Mechanism 1 — Channel Initiation, Seasonal Storm Runoff, & Fluvial Grading (`hydrology.rs`, `climate.rs`, `pipeline.rs`):**
   - Lower `channel_init_threshold` (candidate range `75..140`) and tune MFD convergence (`mfd_exponent_p` `1.85..2.10`) so valley heads initiate cleanly across foothill slopes and caprock benches (`caprock_cv <= 0.45`, `D_d in [0.9, 2.5] km/km²`, `omega_max >= 4`, consistent Hack paths from divide to sea).
   - Add an opt-in `seasonal_storm_runoff_coeff: f32` field on `HydrologyConfig` (default `0.0` so all existing unit tests keep exact `(P - PET).max(0)` semantics; enabled `0.08..0.16` in `pipeline.rs`) yielding physical convective stormflow `c * P * (P / (P + PET))` in seasonal/rain-shadow piedmonts without erasing the humid-to-arid dissection contrast (`windward_dd / plains_dd >= 1.5`), activating `M2` rain-shadow junctions (`rs_n >= 3`) and boosting coastal mouths over `delta_min_q_m3_s = 2.0`.
   - Increase effective fluvial erosion timescale (`fluvial_k_scale` / coarse-to-mid duration) and gentle plateau topographic routing so Fastscape-SD achieves steady-state Flint concavity $\theta \approx 0.45 \in [0.35, 0.65]$, `V`-gorge upstream shapes ($b_{\text{up}} \sim 1.0$) transitioning to wider alluviated/glaciated lower valleys ($b_{\text{down}} \ge 1.6, V_{f,\text{down}} \ge 1.5$), plus mildly enhanced montane windward/ridge moisture (`2200–3500 m`) so connected `Taiga` stays well above `2,500` cells (`16/16` zoned).
2. **Mechanism 2 — Lithologic Caprock Knickpoints & Channel Protection (`tectonic.rs`, `erosion_fluvial.rs`, `erosion_thermal.rs`, `pipeline.rs`):**
   - Structure dipping Mesozoic sandstone strata and plateau margins in `tectonic.rs` so active `Q >= 0.25 m³/s` streams repeatedly traverse `SandstoneCaprock` benches onto `SoftShale` / `SolubleLimestone`.
   - Sharpen lithologic boundaries at fine resolution (no smeared boundary blur across sandstone-over-shale contacts), enhance physically bounded differential shale undercutting below sandstone lips during fine `S2` steps (preserving `K_max / K_min <= 8.0` and solver convergence budgets), and protect active `SandstoneCaprock` stream cells against smoothing collapse during `S3` while capping slopes cleanly under rock repose (`<= 65°`) so waterfalls (`drop >= 12 m` within `<= 30 m`, `up_slope < 0.08`) survive in `[20, 80]` at `4096²`.
3. **Mechanism 3 — Pull-Apart / Foreland Fault Basins & Paleo-Glacial Overdeepening (`tectonic.rs`, `erosion_glacial.rs`, `pipeline.rs`):**
   - Widen and articulate the segmented fault-graben / foreland sag basins and structural relay sills in `tectonic.rs` alongside a broader volcanic caldera basin, and strengthen `GlacialParams` steps/`k_g` above `3,350 m` in `pipeline.rs`. Every basin remains topologically connected to Fill-Spill-Merge outflow (`endo_humid == 0.0`) while raising `lake_surface_fraction` into `[0.008, 0.045]` (`~1.2%–2.2%`) with `large >= 5`, high-páramo cirque lakes, and caldera/trough lakes.
4. **Mechanism 4 — Multi-Octave Fractal Coastline & Headland Sea Cliffs (`tectonic.rs`):**
   - Replace the low-order coastline modulation with a band-limited multi-octave fractal coastline curve spanning `~4 km` peninsulas down to `~60 m` coves/headlands while preserving `west_edge`, `east_third`, and `[3000, 14500] m` massif-to-coast unit bounds, lifting `D_shore` into `[1.15, 1.45]` and `256²` `walkable_shoreline_cells >= 500`.
   - Along `~25–35%` rocky headland sectors (avoiding deltaic shelf mouths), replace the `0.0023` flat coastal ramp with a wave-cut / fault-scarp coastal cliff face (`slope ~ 0.45–0.85` over the shoreline band, far below rim sightlines so `obstructed == 0`), keeping `~65–75%` of bays/beaches gentle (`slope < 0.18`).
5. **Mechanism 5 — Fine-Scale `10 m` Thermal/Talus Relaxation (`pipeline.rs`, `erosion_thermal.rs`):**
   - Restore full fine-level (`lvl_dx < 30.0`) thermal relaxation (`talus_rate = 0.45..0.55`, stable `diffusivity_d = 0.018..0.030`, `substeps = 18..24`) so isolated `> 75°` opposing 1-cell canyon/ridge spikes relax completely below rock repose (`non_cliff_needle_spikes == 0`, `p99.9 < 70°`) without flattening mapped caprock waterfall steps.
