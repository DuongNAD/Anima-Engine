## 2. Root-Cause Chain from Provisional `C01` (`4096²`, `2/15` Pass)

At `4096²` (`dx = 10 m`), provisional `C01` (`0x57563201`) passed `2/15` gates (`G-SHAPE.6.EndorheicSpillways` `endo_humid = 0.0` and `G-COMP-v2.C2.Ecotone` `ecotone = 0.3836`, `jump = 0.00697`) and failed `13/15` gates via five coupled physical deficits (see [the WV2-4 Stage-2 table](2026-11-24-wv2-4.md#L91-L141)):

1. **Channel network truncation (`omega_max = 3`) & ungraded profiles (`G-SHAPE.1..5`, `C4`, `GS-1`):**
   - `pipeline.rs` passed `channel_init_threshold = 500.0` (`hydrology.rs`: `A * S^2 >= 500.0`) with MFD dispersion `mfd_exponent_p = 1.6`. On gentle sandstone caprock tiles (`S ~ 0.015–0.04`) and foothill divides at `dx = 10 m`, streams must collect `0.2–1.0 km²` of dispersed overland area before turning channelized. This leaves flat caprock tiles nearly streamless (`caprock_cv = 1.4983 > 0.45`, `23` tiles), drops global `D_d = 0.5746 km/km²` (`< 0.8`), caps Strahler order at `omega_max = 3`, and starts `M4` channel paths at `longest_m = dx` already mid-basin (`h = 0.4525`, `R^2 = 0.3650`, `max_path = 9.89 km`).
   - Simultaneously, `hydrology.rs` computed runoff strictly as `(precip_m_yr - pet_m_yr).max(0.0)` against tropical lowland `PET ~ 1.35 m/yr`. Rain-shadow zones therefore generated zero local runoff, leaving `M2` rain-shadow junctions at `rs_n = 2 (< 3)`, starving lowland deltas (`deltas = 1 < 2`), and keeping `river_surface_fraction = 0.000250` (`< 0.0005`).
   - Fastscape-SD fluvial evolution reached only `129 kyr` of cumulative time, inverting Flint's Law (`theta = -0.2080`), while low upper-montane moisture left `C01` `Taiga` fragmented at `131` connected cells (`15/16` zoned `< 2,500`).
2. **Zero `4096²` waterfalls (`C1 wf = 0`, `[20, 80]`):**
   - Stage S6 detects knickpoints only on `SandstoneCaprock` (`lithology_id == 1`) cells with `Q >= 0.25 m³/s`, upstream channel slope `< 0.08`, and `>= 12.0 m` vertical drop within `30.0 m` downstream. Bilinear upsampling smeared coarse scarps at `4096²`, fine fluvial steps incised `< 3 m`, and unguarded hillslope relaxation eliminated every qualifying `>= 12 m / 30 m` lip (`wf = 0`).
3. **Under-represented lakes (`C3 lake_frac = 0.000256`, `large = 2` vs `[0.008, 0.045]`, `large >= 5`):**
   - Static graben/caldera depressions were `~31×` below `C3`'s floor or incised open during Stage S2.
4. **Over-smooth coastline & zero coastal cliffs (`M4.2 d_shore = 0.9872`, `C5 cliff = 0.0000`, `GS-2 shoreline_cells = 275`):**
   - Low-order coastline modulation plus a flat `0.00232` coastal ramp produced Euclidean perimeter scaling (`D_shore = 0.9872`), zero `slope >= 0.35` coastal cliff cells (`C5`), and only `275` shoreline cells on `256²` (`GS-2` floor `500`).
5. **`10 m` non-cliff needle spikes (`G-SHAPE.7 spikes = 4,642`, `p99.9 = 78.44°`):**
   - Attenuating fine-level (`lvl_dx < 30.0`) thermal relaxation down to `diffusivity_d = 0.002` and `substeps = 4` failed to clamp steep `10 m` opposing 1-cell trench walls below `75°`.
