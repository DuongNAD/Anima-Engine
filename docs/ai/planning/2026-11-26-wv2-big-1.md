## 2. Physics & Geometry Design Summary (`dx = 20 m` vs `80 m` Findings)

Calibration iterated across `1024^2` (`dx = 80 m`, sweeps `B1..B11`) and two `4096^2` (`dx = 20 m`) runs (`BIG_4096` and final `BIG_4096_v2`), surfacing three critical resolution-dependent mechanisms:

1. **Grid-Spacing `TWI` Shift (`dx = 20 m` vs `80 m`):** Topographic wetness index $\ln(A / \tan\beta)$ drops by $\approx \ln(4) \approx 1.39$ when cell spacing halves twice from `80 m` to `20 m`, shifting median soil moisture downward (`p50: 0.530 -> 0.478`). `biomes.rs` applies `dx_moist_boost = 0.025 * log2((80.0 / dx_m).max(1.0))` on `large_world` (`0.0` at `80 m`, `+0.050` at `20 m`), aligning all five biome groups at both `1024^2` and `4096^2`.
2. **Shoreline Perimeter & Rocky Headland Budget (`C5` + `C5b`):** At `20 m`, eight braided prograding deltas expand total coastline length (`22,002` cells), diluting narrow rocky arcs below the `0.10` `C5` cliff floor when buffered linearly. Switching `tayrona_spur` to a flat-top 4th-power super-Gaussian coastal cliff arc (`exp(-dv^4)` anchored on `d_coast_m`) with headland ria modulation and detaching offshore volcanic islands safely offshore (`-8,200 m / -9,600 m / -7,200 m`) simultaneously satisfies `C5` (`steep_cliff = 0.1041 >= 0.10`, `0` obstructed sightlines) and `WV2-BIG-2` `C5b` (`5 km = 0.7455 >= 0.60`, `3 km = 0.8047 >= 0.75`, `med_1000m = 12.877 km >= 10.0 km`, `front < 4 km = 0.0806 <= 0.20`).
3. **Continental Rain-Shadow Equilibrium (`E2` + `endorheic_humid = 0`):** Smooth `C^1` leeward evaporation-recycling damping (`q_in < 0.78 -> 0.28` damp, `leeward_rain_shadow_gain = 1.32`) maintains an authentic western rain shadow (`Desert + Badlands + Savanna = 19.47%` of land) without introducing humid endorheic artifacts (`endorheic_humid_fraction = 0.0`).
